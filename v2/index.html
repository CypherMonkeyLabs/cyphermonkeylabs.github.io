Sister technologies

  • WFM

    Workforce management covers scheduling and adherence. There is no WFM lab here. The nearest campuses are Amazon Connect, Five9, Genesys Cloud, NICE CXone, and Zoom, Talkdesk and Avaya.

  • Recording / QM

    Call recording and quality management sit beside a contact center. There is no recording lab here. The same contact-center campuses are the practice rooms.

  • E911

    Emergency calling location is a voice-network concern. There is no E911 lab here. The voice campuses are Unified Communications, Microsoft Voice, and Webex.

  • Analytics

    Queue and outcome reports are their own practice. There is no analytics product lab here. BA Lab is where requirements and process maps are practiced. It is not a reporting product.

  • CRM

    Customer records usually sit next to the agent desk. There is no CRM lab here. The contact-center campuses are where routing and the desk are practiced.

  • AI agents

    The about section names AI omni-channel agents as the direction I am heading. AI Lab is the campus for prompts, agents, and tools. Python Lab is the coding studio beside it.

Learning path

From fundamentals to advanced.

Levels come from the lab registry: starter, explorer, navigator. They describe the campus. They are not a certificate, and they are not a required order.

  1. 1

    Fundamentals

    Start where a call, a script, or a requirement begins.

    1. SIP Universe — starter. How a call starts its trip.
    2. Python Lab — starter. From print to projects, in the browser.
    3. BA Lab — starter. Requirements, stories, and process maps.
  2. 2

    Platform practice

    Explorer campuses. Open the one that matches the system you want to study.

    1. Amazon Connect
    2. Five9
    3. Genesys Cloud
    4. NICE CXone
    5. Zoom, Talkdesk and Avaya
    6. Microsoft Voice
    7. CPaaS
    8. AI Lab
  3. 3

    Advanced campuses

    Navigator is the registry’s deepest level today. Two campuses carry it.

    1. Unified Communications Lab — navigator.
    2. Webex Lab — navigator.

    Beside the path, when you want a break from platforms: Sutra Nexus and the Gemini Gems gallery.

About me

I am a lifelong student.

I am Lucky, also known as Cypher Monkey (@Cypher0x9): a lifelong student and an AI builder. I come from systems, networking, voice, and contact center, and I am moving into AI omni-channel agents.

I am learning everything I can: languages, servers, cloud, and the agents that tie it together. Then I share what I learn here, in the open, so the next curious person has a head start.

These are independent educational projects. They are not official training from any vendor, and I keep claims to what I have checked.
