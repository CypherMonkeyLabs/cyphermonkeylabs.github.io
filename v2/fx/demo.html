CMFX demo

Aurora, a mouse-reactive particle field, a 3D lab globe, tilt cards, a cursor trail, and a Web Audio bed. Sound stays off until you turn it on. Move the pointer to wake the field, the cards, and the trail.

Effects are starting.

Aurora

A fullscreen shader. The bands follow the Python Lab studio aurora. The wisps are Ashima simplex noise. Reduced motion holds a single frame.

Particles

Canvas particles drift upward and lean toward the pointer. This path does not need WebGL, so it is also the fallback.

Lab globe

Drag the globe or use the arrow keys after it is focused. Every lab name below is a real link. three.js loads for this section only.

Tilt cards

Fine pointers tilt the card. Touch and reduced motion leave it flat. The cards are links.

Python Lab The 3D studio that the aurora shader came from. Site repo Source for this preview. Linktree Cypher Monkey's public links.

Cursor trail

On a fine pointer, dots fade behind the cursor. The layer ignores clicks. It is not drawn for reduced motion or a touch-only screen.

Sound

The fixed toggle is the only way the bed starts. These buttons call CMFX.sound.ping. They stay silent until sound is on. Kinds: tap, hover, ok, nav, warn.
