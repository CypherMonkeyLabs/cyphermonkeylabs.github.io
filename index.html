About me

I am a lifelong student.

I am Lucky, also known as CypherMonkey: a lifelong student and an AI enthusiast. I come from systems, networking, voice, and contact center, and I am moving into AI omni-channel agents.

I am learning everything I can: languages, servers, cloud, and the agents that tie it together. Then I share what I learn here, in the open, so the next curious person has a head start.

These are independent educational projects. They are not official training from any vendor, and I keep claims to what I have checked.
